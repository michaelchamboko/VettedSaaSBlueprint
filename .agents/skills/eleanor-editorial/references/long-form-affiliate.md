# Long-form affiliate review

Write for a buyer deciding whether the product fits a specific job. Give the buyer a reason to follow the disclosed affiliate link when the product fits. Give a reader facing a disqualifying constraint enough information to walk away.

## Transforming

Record the intended reader, purchase decision, strongest evidenced advantage, disqualifying condition, and action the approved destination supports. Use the evidence behind the advantage and limitation to decide what belongs in the article. Aim for about 2,050 words during drafting. A gap in length calls for a buyer question that the available evidence can answer. Return to research for missing evidence. Move or add sections while the draft structure is still editable.

## Eleanor's three passes

1. **Purpose and structure.** Ask what the buyer learns from each section that helps them decide. Give the answer early. Develop it in a sequence the reader can follow. Use specific situations, comparisons or consequences when the evidence supports them. Return structural repairs to drafting if the protected shape must change.
2. **Meaning and evidence.** Tie each material advantage to a source and a consequence for this buyer. Explain when a limitation changes the recommendation. Preserve price terms, uncertainty, disclosures and the approved destination. Use an example of a constraint only when it is supported or clearly hypothetical.
3. **Wording and rhythm.** Read adjacent paragraphs together. Cut repeated verdicts, stock openings and descriptions that could apply to any competing product. Keep technical detail when it changes the decision. Read the recommendation alongside its link text and check what the buyer can actually do at that destination.

## Independent review and final approval

Read the whole article in order. Cite the actual passages that establish who should use the product, the strongest supported reason to choose it, when to choose something else, and what the affiliate link lets the reader do or verify next. Explain why those passages answer the brief. Trace commercial claims to the retained evidence. Check the action promised in the copy against the approved link. A visit to a vendor homepage alone supports no promise of a price, trial, discount or purchase outcome.

Report duplicated arguments, filler written to reach the target, unsupported claims, misleading calls to action and unresolved objections. Require a repair when a paragraph adds length without helping the decision, or when the reader has to supply a missing step in the reasoning. A clean scanner score alone cannot settle those questions.

Measure the final reader-visible article with the shared counter. Accept 1,800 through 2,300 words, inclusive. Keep navigation, metadata, URLs, repeated buttons and boilerplate disclosure outside the count. Preserve the disclosure on the page. Bind the judgment and count to the final article so later edits require fresh evidence.
