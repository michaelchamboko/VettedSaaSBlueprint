## Bounded inference and uncertainty

A limited localization pilot is reasonable when the business can identify an audience need, provide fluent review, correct the output and verify its intended production use. Tool purchase is conditional on a requirement the native workflow cannot meet. Native YouTube and commissioned human localization remain valid alternatives.

The YouTube adoption signal is not an independent experiment and cannot predict this audience's response. Vendor documentation is not proof of translation quality. Model distinctions and commercial exclusions are material limitations shown beside the recommendation. No claim of legal suitability, guaranteed revenue, cheapest price, universal ranking or measured savings is made.

Scenario assumptions are explicitly fictional/illustrative: an existing English tutorial, Spanish-speaking audience requests, content permission and fluent reviewer access. There is no invented customer case study.

Follow-up: refresh model editing access, beta/production eligibility, plan conditions and channel language availability before revising this guide. Gather real pilot feedback before expanding a recommendation.
