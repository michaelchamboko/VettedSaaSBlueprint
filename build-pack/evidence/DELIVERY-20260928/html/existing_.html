How we vet

A review should make a decision clearer, not louder.

  1. 01

    Evidence before adjectives

    Claims need a traceable source. If the evidence is thin, the wording stays modest.

  2. 02

    Fit over popularity

    We focus on the buyer, workflow, and constraint a product serves, not a generic score.

  3. 03

    Limits stay visible

    Important tradeoffs sit beside the recommendation, not in fine print at the bottom.

  4. 04

    Human approval

    Every public review passes evidence, compliance, and owner checks before it appears here.
