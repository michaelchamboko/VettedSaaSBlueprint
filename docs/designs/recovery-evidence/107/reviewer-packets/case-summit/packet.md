# Independent review: case-summit

Review only this packet. Do not inspect sibling case directories or the evaluation directory. Return your judgment before discussing it with another reviewer.

## Criterion

Source: *Vetted SaaS Blueprint — Master Product Requirements Document*, §29.8 “Editorial Quality”, stable source `D05-S-92035809db89`.

An editorial rewrite must use plain English, consistent terminology, and factual restraint while preserving the supplied statement’s semantic meaning. A smoother sentence is not acceptable if it changes the decision question.

## Reviewable evidence

- Supplied statement: **Which account and project should be open right now?**
- Rewritten statement: **Which account and project needs your attention now?**
- Context: the sentence helps a reader check whether their current desktop workspace makes the active account and project clear after an interruption
- No new evidence or owner instruction authorizes a change in the decision question

## Required response

Return all three fields:

- **Observed evidence:** the semantic relationship you observed between the supplied and rewritten statements.
- **Verdict:** `meets` or `does_not_meet`.
- **Explanation:** why the rewrite does or does not preserve the original decision question.
