## Criterion

Source: *Vetted SaaS Blueprint — Design System and Experience Standards*, §21.4 “CTA language”, stable source `D04-S-1497bdd3519d`.

A CTA must make clear what will happen, what value the visitor receives, whether the click is an internal step or an external provider visit, and whether clicking creates a commitment.

## Reviewable evidence

- Visible label: **Visit WebCatalog’s vendor site**
- Supporting text: **Compare current plans and product details before deciding.**
- Resolved destination: `https://vendor.example/products/webcatalog`
- Destination type: external vendor page
- Commercial relationship: affiliate destination; the page displays its affiliate disclosure above this action
- Clicking opens the destination in the current tab and creates no purchase or account commitment by itself
